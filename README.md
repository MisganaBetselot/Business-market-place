# Business Marketplace

## Overview

Business Marketplace is a web-based platform designed for **buying and selling existing businesses**. Business owners can create listings for businesses they want to sell, while potential buyers can browse available businesses, view their details, and contact sellers through the platform.

The platform uses a **subscription-based model for sellers**, while buyers can browse and interact with business listings.

## Core Features

- User registration and JWT authentication
- Business listing creation and management
- Business categories
- Seller subscription plans
- Payment receipt submission and admin approval
- Photo and video uploads
- Media review and moderation
- Buyer inquiries
- User notifications
- Business and media reporting
- Admin review and management
- Administrative audit logs
- Subscription expiration and notification system

## Main Workflow

```text
Seller Registration
        ↓
Choose Subscription
        ↓
Submit Payment Receipt
        ↓
Admin Approval
        ↓
Create Business Listing
        ↓
Upload Photos / Videos
        ↓
Admin Media Review
        ↓
Listing Goes Live
        ↓
Buyers Browse & Send Inquiries
```
